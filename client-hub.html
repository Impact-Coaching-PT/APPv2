About Ritchie

I'm Ritchie, and I've been a personal trainer since 2018 - though if you'd told me back then I'd end up doing this for a living, I wouldn't have believed you.

I didn't plan any of this. I went through my own weight loss journey, just diet and training, figuring it out for myself. Somewhere along the way people around me started asking what I was doing and how they could do it too. I ended up helping a few friends, then a few more, and eventually someone just said "why don't you do this properly, as a coach?" So I did. I think that's part of why I do this the way I do - I've actually been on the other side of it.

Qualifications & experience
Level 3 Personal Trainer, with specialist training in menopause fitness, GLP-1 and medical weight loss support, adolescent training, group instruction, and strength training.

My approach
I'm not a shouty, drill-sergeant type coach, and I never will be. My job is to support people through what they're doing and push them to their own limits - not mine, not some generic standard. I keep things simple, no confusing exercise jargon or overcomplicated explanations of movements, and I'm autistic myself, which if anything I think helps me explain things clearly and read what a client actually needs rather than following a script.

I've got clients I've worked with for years, and I love that. But if I've done my job properly, you shouldn't need me forever - my real goal with anyone I train is to get them to the point where they've got the knowledge and confidence to do it on their own.
